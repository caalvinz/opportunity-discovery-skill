# Commercial judgment lenses

## Problem and value
Who has the problem? How frequently? What does failure cost? What is the existing workaround? Is the problem urgent enough to change behavior? What outcome is observable?

## Adoption and distribution
Differentiate end user, beneficiary, buyer, budget owner and champion. An existing membership channel reduces distribution friction **only as a hypothesis**; it does not prove activation, repeated use, renewal or retention.

## Startup economics
Assess reachability, substitutes, differentiation, acquisition effort, conversion, pricing, service costs, support, renewal and gross margin. Use ranges only with credible inputs.

## Internal product economics
Assess workflow baseline, labor reallocation (not just time saved), change-management, integration, maintenance, governance, failure cost, and executive sponsor.

## Strategic fit
Why this organization? Why now? What can be tested cheaply? What would be displaced by this investment?

## AI versus non-AI
Compare doing nothing, playbooks, training, existing SaaS, simple workflow software, AI copilot and autonomous agent. For sensitive HR decisions, keep professional judgment and policy interpretation under accountable human control.

## Gate
Pursue / Experiment / Park / Reject. Evidence needed should scale with investment size and reversibility.
