# Evidence standards

- **Observed**: first-hand measured/seen episode, with method, date and context.
- **Reported**: what a practitioner, user, expert, publication or study says. Attribution matters.
- **Inferred**: reasoned conclusion, with competing explanation.
- **Assumed**: untested proposition.

An expert's observations about a group are *reported evidence* to us, not direct observation of all intended users. A user's stated preference is not a purchase commitment. A convincing conversation is not market validation.

For important claims record: hypothesis ID; source/date; segment; observation/method; supporting and contradictory evidence; limitations; decision implication. Prefer primary evidence and behavior over hypothetical willingness. Do not invent numerical certainty or empirical thresholds.

Before experiments, pre-register: target hypothesis; rival explanation; method and baseline; target participants; observable success and failure; budget/time limit; interpretation of ambiguous results; what decision each outcome changes.
